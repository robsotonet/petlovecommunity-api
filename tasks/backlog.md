## Backlog Management

### Prioritization Criteria
1. **Business Value**: Impact on user experience and revenue
2. **Technical Risk**: Complexity and dependencies
3. **User Demand**: Requests from users and stakeholders
4. **Market Competition**: Features available in competing platforms
5. **Technical Debt**: Impact on system maintenance and scalability

### Estimation Guidelines
- **Small**: 1-3 days (1-3 story points)
- **Medium**: 1-2 weeks (5-8 story points)
- **Large**: 2-4 weeks (13-21 story points)
- **Extra Large**: 1+ months (34+ story points)

### Review Schedule
- **Monthly**: Review priorities and add new items
- **Quarterly**: Major feature planning and roadmap updates
- **Annually**: Strategic backlog review and technical debt assessment

### Backlog Health Metrics
- **Total Items**: 25+ features planned
- **Ready for Development**: Items with clear requirements
- **Technical Debt Ratio**: <20% of total backlog
- **Innovation vs Maintenance**: 70% features, 30% improvements
